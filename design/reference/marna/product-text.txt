FAQ
What’s the best way to brew loose leaf tea?
You’ll find brewing guides specific to each blend on the product pages, including water temperature, steeping time, and tips for getting the best flavour.
Do I need special equipment to make loose leaf tea?
Not at all. A simple tea strainer or infuser is all you need. Our Marna Tea Club
includes everything required to start your loose leaf tea ritual. No mess, no fuss.
How should I store my loose leaf tea?
Keep your tea in a cool, dry place , away from direct sunlight and moisture - our resealable pouches help preserve freshness.
What’s the shelf life of loose leaf tea?
Our teas stay fresh for 12–18 months if stored correctly. While tea doesn’t ‘go bad’, the flavour is best enjoyed within this timeframe.
Are your loose leaf teas caffeine-free?
Some of our blends are naturally caffeine-free, like our Chamomile Sweet Dreams , whilst others, like our Darjeeling Black , contain natural caffeine from tea leaves. You’ll find caffeine guides specific to each blend on each product page. To discover our tea blends, visit /collections/teas
Are your teas organic?
Tea is a category that doesn’t fit neatly into the organic certification model , which is why you’ll rarely see high-quality tea brands carrying the organic label.
When we built Marna’s supply chain, we had two choices:
1. Source from large, mass-market suppliers that could afford organic certification,
but often underpay tea pickers and have fewer controls on quality, or
2. Partner directly with smaller, specialist farms where we can ensure that the tea is
grown and picked responsibly, ethically, and to the highest possible standard.
We chose the second route.
Every tea we use is carefully sourced for quality and fairness , even if it doesn’t carry an official organic stamp. In fact, the whole tea leaves we brew with are of higher grade than the tea sold in many locations across the world. The organic label is valuable for certain products, but in tea it can be misleading.
What matters most is how the tea is grown, picked, and processed, and we take that responsibility very seriously.
What makes Marna loose leaf tea different from tea bags?
Tea bags often contain broken tea ‘dust’ and can even release microplastics into yourdrink. Our loose leaf teas use whole, carefully sourced leaves for maximum flavour, quality, andhealth benefits - plus, they’re better for the environment.
